# Example results

InsightMIP and per-scan MIPs of three lymphoma patients, produced by this pipeline.

## Data and licence

The images are derived from the FDG-PET-CT-Lesions (autoPET) dataset of The Cancer Imaging Archive, licensed under
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). They were changed: they are maximum intensity projections
with the pipeline's lesion segmentation, not the original images.

- Gatidis S, Kuestner T. A whole-body FDG-PET/CT dataset with manually annotated tumor lesions (FDG-PET-CT-Lesions).
  The Cancer Imaging Archive, 2022. https://doi.org/10.7937/gkr0-xv29
- Gatidis S, et al. A whole-body FDG-PET/CT Dataset with manually annotated Tumor Lesions. Scientific Data 9, 601 (2022).
  https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-022-01718-3
